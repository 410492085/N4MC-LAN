EasyTier 2.6.4 for Windows x64 is included as a separate executable component.
The `easytier-core.exe` and `easytier-cli.exe` files are unchanged from the
[official binary release](https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/releases/tag/v2.6.4).
The [corresponding source archive](https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/archive/refs/tags/v2.6.4.zip)
is available from EasyTier. See `EasyTier-LGPL-3.0.txt` and `Qt-GPL-3.0.txt`
for the LGPL and GPL license texts.

The included x64 `Packet.dll` is from the [WinPcap 4.1.3 distribution](https://www.winpcap.org/install/).
See `WinPcap-LICENSE.html` for its copyright notices and terms.
